The big picture

Three phases from raw land to pad-ready

Every lot goes through the same three phases before a home can go up on it.

1
Phase 1

Entitlements

Get the government to say yes: zoning, site plan, public hearings.

2
Phase 2

Engineering

Civil engineers lay out grading, drainage, roads, and utility stubs.

3
Phase 3

Site Work

Clear, grade, install utilities and roads, and build the pads.

Before you buy

The one variable that moves everything

  • A lot with mains at the street is a completely different budget than one needing a 300-foot extension.
  • North Georgia granite can add $5,000–$20,000+ in blasting if you hit rock.

Bottom line: Budget $25K–$50K per lot for horizontal work in Georgia, and verify utility distance and soil/rock conditions before you buy.
